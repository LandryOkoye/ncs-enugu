Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 29 July 2026
Last Updated: 29 July 2026
1. Introduction
The Nigeria Computer Society (NCS), Enugu State Chapter (“NCS Enugu”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of every visitor, member, partner, sponsor, nominee, applicant, and participant who interacts with our website.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or engage with our programmes, events, awards, trainings, publications, partnerships, and other activities.
By accessing or using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope
This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through:
The official NCS Enugu website;


Online registration forms;


Event and conference registrations;


Award nominations and applications;


Public voting platforms;


Sponsorship and partnership enquiries;


Membership interest forms;


Newsletter subscriptions;


Surveys and feedback forms;


Email correspondence; and


Any other digital services operated by NCS Enugu.



3. Our Commitment to Data Protection
NCS Enugu processes personal information responsibly and in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and other applicable data protection and privacy laws.
We are committed to processing personal information lawfully, fairly, transparently, and securely.

4. Information We Collect
Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following categories of information.
Personal Information
This may include:
Full name


Email address


Telephone number


Residential or business address (where required)


Organisation or institution


Job title or designation


Professional profile


Educational institution


Social media handles (where voluntarily provided)


Profile photographs (where submitted)


Event Information
Where you register for an event, we may collect:
Registration details


Attendance preferences


Dietary or accessibility requirements (where applicable)


Emergency contact information (where necessary)


Award Information
Where you submit or are nominated for an award, we may collect:
Biography


Professional achievements


Supporting documents


Links to projects or portfolios


Public profile information


References or endorsements


Sponsorship and Partnership Information
Where organisations engage with us, we may collect:
Company details


Contact persons


Business correspondence


Proposal documents


Technical Information
When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:
IP address


Browser type


Operating system


Device type


Pages visited


Date and time of access


Referring website


Website usage statistics



5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect
We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a legitimate purpose and you have voluntarily provided it or applicable law permits us to process it.
Where special categories of personal data become necessary, appropriate safeguards will be implemented.

6. How We Collect Information
We collect information through:
Forms completed on our website;


Event registrations;


Award nomination portals;


Voting systems;


Email correspondence;


Newsletter subscriptions;


Surveys;


Cookies and analytics technologies; and


Direct communication with NCS Enugu.



7. Legal Basis for Processing
We process personal information only where we have a lawful basis to do so, including:
Your consent;


Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps;


Compliance with legal obligations;


Legitimate interests pursued by NCS Enugu, provided such interests do not override your rights and freedoms;


Protection of vital interests where applicable.



8. How We Use Your Information
Your information may be used to:
Process event registrations;


Evaluate award nominations and applications;


Administer public voting;


Verify submitted information;


Communicate with applicants, nominees, sponsors, partners, and attendees;


Respond to enquiries;


Send event updates and important announcements;


Deliver newsletters where you have subscribed;


Improve our website and digital services;


Conduct statistical analysis;


Maintain website security;


Prevent fraud and abuse;


Comply with legal obligations.


We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

9. Email Communications
Where you have opted in or where communication is necessary for an event or service you requested, we may send emails relating to:
Event updates


Registration confirmations


Invitations


Award notifications


Partnership information


Newsletters


Important organisational announcements


You may unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time. Administrative communications relating to your registration or participation may still be sent where necessary.

10. Cookies and Analytics
Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:
Remember user preferences;


Improve website performance;


Understand visitor behaviour;


Measure website traffic;


Enhance user experience.


You may disable cookies through your browser settings. However, doing so may affect certain website functionality.

11. Sharing of Personal Information
We may disclose personal information only where necessary to:
Technology service providers;


Website hosting providers;


Event management platforms;


Email communication platforms;


Payment processors (where applicable);


Professional advisers;


Government agencies or regulators where legally required.


All third parties engaged by NCS Enugu are expected to implement appropriate security and confidentiality measures.
We do not sell or rent personal information.

12. International Data Transfers
Some of our technology providers may store or process information outside Nigeria.
Where personal information is transferred internationally, NCS Enugu will take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are implemented and that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

13. Data Retention
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:
Provide requested services;


Administer events and programmes;


Maintain organisational records;


Resolve disputes;


Comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting obligations.


When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted, anonymised, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable laws and internal policies.



14. Data Security
NCS Enugu maintains appropriate administrative, organisational, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information against:
Unauthorised access;


Loss;


Misuse;


Alteration;


Disclosure; and


Destruction.


Despite these measures, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users therefore provide information at their own discretion.

15. Your Privacy Rights
Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:
Request access to your personal information;


Request correction of inaccurate information;


Request deletion of your information where legally permissible;


Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;


Object to certain processing activities;


Request restriction of processing;


Request information regarding how your data is processed;


Lodge a complaint with the appropriate regulatory authority where applicable.


Requests will be considered and responded to within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.
16. Children’s Privacy
Our website and services are primarily intended for adults and higher education students.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law.

17. Third-Party Websites
Our website may contain links to third-party websites.
NCS Enugu does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, policies, or content of external websites. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites they visit.

Event reporting;
